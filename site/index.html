Install in a minute

Download Soothia (.zip)
  1. Download and unzip the file.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped soothia folder.
  5. Pin Soothia, open a match page, and look for the orb bottom right.
