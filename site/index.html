Install in a minute

Download Soothia (.zip)
  1. Download and unzip the file.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped soothia folder.
  5. Pin Soothia, open any post on X, and look for the orb bottom right.

You'll need a Solana wallet (Phantom, Jupiter, Solflare or Backpack) with a little USDC and SOL to create markets or bet. Chrome Web Store listing coming soon.
